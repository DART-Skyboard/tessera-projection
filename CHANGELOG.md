# Changelog

## 0.5.0
Release candidate. Highlights since 0.4.1:
- **Projection Remesh: Symmetrize.** Truly mirror-symmetric quad output (choose the trusted side and a strength), with a before/after asymmetry report; REDUCE mirrors exactly, also for planes off the origin.
- **Fluid mode: new fluid behaviour.** Gas pressure with two-way coupling to liquids and walls, transmutation (water to steam, crystals growing from nuclei, burning, rust), a smooth merged liquid surface, soft volume gases (steam, smoke), viscosity and emission surfaces, Add Fluid Volume, and a generalised assignment model (any geometry as Container or Shape, spawn modes, primitives, several independent assets).
- **Shader presets.** 22 Cycles + EEVEE materials (water, glass, oil, honey, lava, smoke, steam, flame ...) with exposed parameters and a Shader presets panel.
- **Wind tunnel and thermal readback.** A wind tunnel builder (atmosphere presets for Earth, Mars, Venus, Titan, Moon), plain collision bodies, and surface temperature written to attributes and colour with an optional colour bar.
- **Combustion.** Per-engine chain (channels and chambers form engines with their own throat, choked flow and thrust), exact reaction pairing, a Smooth surface render type for combustion liquids.
- **Geometry Nodes.** Reusable Tessera node groups (source, particle cloud, surface, thermal, environment, spawn, viscosity, emission, fluid volume) and a Tessera entry in the Add menu.
- **Collision field.** Triangle BVH and a sparse brick store make large collision meshes practical (see the numbers below); adaptive resolution.
- **UI.** A collapsed Examples panel at the top of the Tessera tab, every other panel opens expanded; the word "demo" is gone from the interface.
- **Release checks.** Every test runs on Blender 5.2.3 and 5.3.0 and from the built extension; a new test draws every panel headlessly (`tests/test_panels_draw.py`) and checks icons, properties, operators and that every Example loads.
- Fixed: the Shader presets panel opened collapsed, unlike the rest of the Tessera tab.

Details by area:
- Fluid mode: gases now render as a soft volume by default (new gas fluids use Ray marching, never beads); transmuted steam / smoke / flame parcels are drawn through the volume path (Points to Volume, AF_Volume material); steam stills re-rendered (`docs/renders/transmute_steam_f85|f120|f150.png`, portrait, from saved states via `docs/render_from_state.py`).
- Fluid mode: **transmutation** (`core/phase.py`). A JSON rule table (`"phase": [...]` next to `species` in the scene's Species rules) moves composition between species over time with animated blending (colour, density, heat capacity interpolate, no pop): GAS (liquid to steam / flame / smoke: the particle becomes a gas parcel at 50 % with hysteresis, rises and carries pressure), CRYSTAL (solid crystals grow shell by shell from nucleation points at the container walls, frozen in place, melt again above the window) and CHEM (first-order oxidation, rust tint, burning). Temperature / pressure / density windows, latent heat `q` with exact enthalpy bookkeeping (mass relabelled 1:1), presets `water_steam`, `wax`, `fuel_flame_smoke`, `salt_crystal`, `rust` (`phase.preset`, `phase.to_json`). Blender: transmuted gas is drawn as soft splats (`AFFMG_Phase`), crystals as flat-shaded faceted shards that grow with the solid fraction (`AFFMC_Crystals`), the liquid surface excludes both; examples `load_steam`, `load_crystal`; render scripts `docs/render_fluid.py steam|crystal`, `docs/render_from_state.py`. Tests: `test_phase` (mass, enthalpy, smooth fractions, crystal growth, melt, rust, JSON round trip).
- Tests: surface-to-body link (`tests/test_surfaces_bpy.py`): a viscosity surface on a dynamic body is linked and the fluid's drag moves the Blender object.
- Fluid mode: **generalised assignment model** (`spawn.py`). A **Container** accepts any geometry (mesh, curve, surface, text, metaball); a **Shape** accepts those plus point clouds and any object with a particle system. Curves (and edge-only meshes) are **channels**: tubed to the object's *Channel radius* (or their own bevel when they have one), capped for a Shape, open-ended for a Container (pipes). Shapes spawn by **Spawn mode**: Auto, Volume fill, Surface, Vertices / points (a channel: points along its length) and Particle system points (a fluid particle at every live Blender particle), with a **Density** control (fraction of the generated particles kept). Every new fluid is a **Primitive** shape (Sphere of points by default; Cube, Cylinder, Cone, Torus, Plane) with size, location, rotation and scale; *Make Editable Object* turns it into a mesh the user edits, or the user picks any object instead. Geometry Nodes: group **Tessera Fluid Spawn** (primitive or shape, spawn mode, spacing, density, seed) and the per-fluid group sockets Spawn Mode, Spawn Density, Use Primitive, Primitive, Primitive Size mirrored from the panel.
- Example: **Three independent assets** (Examples panel): three fluid assets that the Systems list detects as three independent systems and that mix, react (blue + yellow -> green), exchange heat and blend into one smooth Fluid surface when they meet. Tests: `tests/test_assign_blender.py`; renders `docs/render_assign.py`, `docs/renders/assign_*.png`.
- Combustion: **per-engine chain.** Channels and chambers that open into each other form an engine group with its own throat, exit, neck pressure, choked flow, thrust and wall jet (twin-engine examples run both engines through fill, ignition, choking, expansion, ground flattening, smoke and domain fade); every channel can burn like a chamber; reaction pairing uses exact cell keys (fixes distant particles pairing by hash collision). New render type **Smooth surface** for combustion liquids (merged surface + shader preset). Tests `test_combustion_groups.py`, `test_combustion_twin.py`; render `docs/renders/combustion_chain_good_f*.png`.
- Collision field engine (`core/sdf.py`): **triangle BVH** (Morton-sorted, breadth-first batched traversal with an exact upper bound) behind `signed_distance` and the new `closest_surface`; sign still from angle-weighted pseudo-normals, results identical to the old chunk-pruned brute force (kept as `signed_distance_brute`, used for tiny soups). 2000 near-surface queries on a 99k-triangle mesh: 54 s -> 0.24 s CPU; `PartField` build (resolution 40) on 99k triangles: 703 s -> 10-18 s; 1M triangles builds in 29 s (old code did not finish within the 8 GB limit). **Sparse brick store**: refined bricks live in a sorted key array plus a recycled slot pool (no dense cell-sized index) and can now be removed (`remove_bricks`, `coarsen`, `trim_bricks`). **`BrickGrid`**: narrow-band 8^3 brick SDF (bricks only within +-3 voxels, add / remove / trim, 25 % of the dense float64 memory at 128^3). **Adaptive resolution**: `PartField(..., adaptive=True)` runs the website's p90 error probe in voxel units (refine x1.25 above 0.75, coarsen /1.15 below 0.15, scale 0.6-2.0) and reports `resolution_chosen`, `probe_p90`, `probe_history`. Tests: `tests/test_sdf_index.py`.
- Fluid mode: **two-way gas pressure.** Every gas parcel carries the ideal-gas pressure p = rho_sph R T / M of its own state (kernel density against the density it would have at ambient pressure and its own temperature, so p = P_amb x ratio) and pushes its neighbours with the symmetric SPH pair force f = -m_i m_j (p_i/rho_i^2 + p_j/rho_j^2) grad W: hot gas expands, compressed gas pushes, gas parcels of different assets and neighbouring liquid particles feel each other (the pair conserves momentum, checked to 1e-14 N net). The pressure acts on the walls it touches (excess pressure x the parcel's share of the area, along the wall normal), so it moves free and constrained bodies (piston, free block). The gas pressure is softened (`gas_stiff`, a reduced speed of sound as in weakly compressible SPH), one-sided with a 30 % dead band, and the expansion is resisted by ambient drag relative to the asset's own motion. Pressure is a solver output (`S.pressure`, Pa, per particle; liquids: projection pressure + ambient) written to the point attribute `fm_pressure_solver`, which the Geometry Nodes `fm_pressure` now prefers over its estimate.
- Fluid mode: **viscosity surface is bidirectional.** A surface on a dynamic body (Blender: the surface object is a two-way body) drags the fluid and the fluid's drag and excess pressure act back on the body as force and torque about its centre of mass (explicit, one frame of lag), moving it when it is free or constrained (slider, hinge).
- Fluid mode: **smooth merged liquid surface.** Liquids render by default as one continuously updated surface (`SurfaceBuilder`: trilinear splat, Gaussian blur, EMA temporal blend, surface nets, density-weighted vertex colour), so touching blobs of different fluids join with a neck and blend colour across the seam, with no flicker. The glass-of-colours stills are re-rendered with it.
- Tests: `test_gas_pressure`, `test_surface_coupling`, `test_surface_builder` in `tests/test_fluidmode.py`.
- **Shader presets** (`shader_presets.py`, `shader_ops.py`): 22 node-built, Cycles + EEVEE materials (Water, Sea water, Glass, Thin-wall glass, Oil, Honey, Syrup, Milk / cream, Wax, Lava / magma, Mercury, Molten metal, Slime / goo, Blood, Ice, Crystal, Smoke, Steam, Soot, Flame, Plasma, Gas cloud) with exposed parameters (`TS_*` nodes: colour, absorption density, IOR, roughness, subsurface, emission ...), attribute inputs (`fm_color` tint, `fm_temperature` blackbody / crust / glow, `fm_density` scaling) and variants: full (refraction + absorption volume), particle (no refraction, volumes add up), preview (Principled only) and grid (OpenVDB `density` / `temperature`). Socket names are resolved at runtime (4.x / 5.x). New *Shader presets* panel: preset dropdown, assign to selected / active, create from preset, preview mode, Cycles shadow-caustics set-up, per-fluid shader (Auto = default of the fluid preset), assign preset to selected fluid(s); works on meshes, curves, text, volumes. Examples use the shaders (thin-wall glass vessels, water, wax). Tests `tests/test_shaders_bpy.py`; renders `docs/render_shaders.py`, `docs/renders/shaders_*.png`.
- Fluid mode: **Thermal readback** (`thermal.py`, `core/thermal.py`). Select an object (or its faces in Edit Mode, *Use Selected Faces*) of a collision body and enable it: every point or face carries a wall temperature that follows the solver particles around it (kernel probe, recovery temperature `T + r v^2 / 2cp`, flat-plate convection with a natural-convection floor, radiation from heat sources with inverse-square and facing factor, stagnation heating of windward faces, lumped wall heat capacity, re-radiation, exact exponential step). It is written each frame to the `af_temperature` attribute (point or face domain) and to a colour attribute `af_thermal_color` (Solid viewport, colour: Attribute); the shader (colour ramp, range min / max, glow) shows it in EEVEE and Cycles, and an optional Geometry Nodes modifier / group `Tessera Thermal Surface (Point|Face)` outputs the normalised value, the ramp colour and min / mean / max. Per-object toggle, ramp (Thermal default: deep blue, cyan, green, yellow, orange, red, white-hot; Inferno, Turbo, Cold to hot, Grayscale), an optional colour bar legend (gradient plane and K / C labels parented to the camera), absorptivity, wall heat capacity, probe radius and time scale. *Heat the fluid* gives the exchanged heat back to the particles as heat sources. Moving and deforming bodies are read from the evaluated mesh; scrubbing reuses the per-frame cache.
- Fluid mode: **Wind tunnel** builder (`windtunnel.py`): a hollow box with a real wall thickness (open ends or closed) as the Environment container, a Wind Emitter (an Emission surface at the inlet, rate capped) plus an air slug that fills the test section, atmosphere from the Blender scene, an assigned container or a preset (Earth, Mars, Venus, Titan, Moon, vacuum, custom gas with pressure, temperature, gravity and compound; the Air fluid gets its density, viscosity, cp and conductivity from the gas). *Add Collision Body* / *Add Plain Body*: a **plain collision body** only responds to the fluid (no heat source, no thermal readback, no Systems role). Moving parts (object animation) are moving colliders; shape-key / armature deformers re-project their collision field every frame. Parts with Container / Channel / Chamber roles inside the tunnel stay independent Systems.
- Example: **Example: Wind tunnel** (Examples panel): the tunnel, the wind and a tilt-rotor VTOL with spinning rotors, engine exhaust heat sources and hot exhaust emitters and thermal readback on its skin. The DART Skyboard game's VTOL could not be reached from the available repositories (the only Skyboard asset there is an unrelated part), so the aircraft is a procedural stand-in (`assets/vtol.py`, about 2000 vertices, no binary data).
- Tests: `tests/test_thermal.py` (NumPy), `tests/test_windtunnel_bpy.py`; renders `docs/render_windtunnel.py`, `docs/renders/windtunnel_*.png`.
- Fluid mode: **Viscosity surface** and **Emission surface** sources. Buttons *Use selected object*, *Edit faces* (Edit Mode, stored faces preselected) and *Apply* (stores a boolean face attribute). Viscosity: tangential drag of the adjacent fluid with a viscosity coefficient and no-slip fraction, moving surfaces drag the fluid, reaction force read back. Emission: particles/s along the face normal with speed, spread cone, temperature, fluid preset, start / stop, cap; the solver grows its arrays and snapshots stay exact (scrubbing, Reset).
- Fluid mode: **Add Fluid Volume** (non-rendering wireframe-box Empty, scale it with the manipulators): quantity, density, pressure, temperature and preset; free-floating gas / fluid that interacts with the Environment from the first frame.
- Geometry Nodes: `Tessera Viscosity Surface`, `Tessera Emission Surface` and `Tessera Fluid Volume` groups with input / output sockets (read-backs included).
- UI: collapsed **Examples** panel at the top of the Tessera tab with every loadable example (others open expanded); the word 'demo' is replaced by 'example' in all labels and docs (operator ids unchanged). New examples: emission nozzle on a viscous belt, hot and cool gas volumes.
- Tests: `tests/test_surfaces.py`, `tests/test_surfaces_bpy.py`; renders `docs/render_surfaces.py`, `docs/renders/surfaces_*.png`.
- Geometry Nodes parity: five reusable node groups (Tessera Fluid Source, Particle Cloud, Surface, Thermal, Environment) that carry the fluid features as plain geometry and attributes (`fm_velocity`, `fm_temperature`, `fm_pressure`, `fm_density`, `fm_mass`, `fm_color`, `fm_fluid_id`, `fm_viscosity`, `fm_cohesion`, `fm_mix_rate`, `fm_reaction`, `fm_emission`, `fm_flame_color`, ambient attributes), with density volume and welded surface mesh outputs; usable in any node tree, including other extensions' groups.
- The per-fluid group `Tessera Fluid <name>` now has every fluid input (density, mass, temperature, velocity, viscosity, cohesion, mixing rate, heat, reactions, environment, render mode, scale) and outputs (points with attributes, density volume, surface, statistics); existing per-fluid groups are rebuilt once.
- Add menu entry "Tessera" in the Geometry Nodes editor, panel buttons (node editor sidebar and 3D view Tessera tab), library `arc_flow/library/tessera_nodes.blend` (`tools/build_node_library.py`).
- Tests: `tests/test_gn_blender.py`, `tests/test_gn_fluid_blender.py` (stock-node chain and a second extension-style group evaluated headless on 5.2.3 and 5.3.0).
- Projection Remesh: **Symmetrize**. When the detected mirror plane has enough confidence (default on, minimum confidence 0.5) the quad output is made truly mirror-symmetric even if the source or its triangulation is not: choose the trusted side (Auto = the side with more detail, Left, Right, Average) and a strength 0..1; matched vertex pairs, seam vertices exactly on the plane, leftover triangles at the seam get a second tolerant join, every vertex stays projected on the original surface. The operator reports mirror asymmetry before/after (max/mean, fraction of the bounding diagonal) and the deviation from the source.
- Large meshes: PAIR symmetrizes positions and snaps the seam (topology follows the source triangulation, not mirror-paired); REDUCE is an exact topological mirror, now also for planes off the origin or tilted (the reference surface is taken into the plane frame).

## 0.4.1
- Combustion mode (Burn propellants, default on): fuel and oxidiser particles that meet in a chamber react into flame gas (igniter assumed, or autoignition temperature); the pair's O/F sets soot and chemiluminescence through the web app's flame model, ported to `core/flame.py` and checked against the JavaScript (reference values from node in `tests/flame_reference.json`).
- Chamber gas ledger: p_c = m R T / V, drained at the choked throat mass flow (subsonic below the critical pressure ratio); the nozzle exit only passes that mass; gas speed follows the isentropic area-Mach relation (sonic at the throat, supersonic after), scaled to the world speed limit (physical numbers reported).
- Ground impact (wall jet), flame to smoke conversion below 950 K with the flame model's smoke colours, Exhaust domain radius (300 m default, fade over the outer quarter, removal at the radius), Particle budget, Continuous flow ON refills tanks / OFF runs them dry and the plume ends.
- Shared tank-fill selection (`fillsettle.level_select`) for Fluid mode and combustion tanks; Fill / Settle stays a Fluid-mode operator.
- Render: flame / smoke colour and fade attributes (`af_fcol`, `af_fmix`, `af_alpha`) in the Gaussian splat material; time sequence `docs/render_combustion.py`.
- Tests: `tests/test_flame.py`, `tests/test_combustion_chain.py`. Limits in docs/TECHNICAL.md.

## 0.4.0
- Fluid mode: composition model (`core/species.py`): species mass fractions per particle, miscible Fick exchange (mass and enthalpy conserved), subtractive colour mixing, ideal-mixing density, mass-weighted cp and beta, ABSORB with priority and cluster ids, REACT rules with windows and heat release, JSON rule table.
- New render mode Surface (surface nets on a smoothed particle density, welded coloured mesh); Colour Mixing demo with a rotating paddle; per-fluid mix rate, priority and rules in the panel.
- Tests: mixing, mass and heat conservation, dab fade, ABSORB, REACT, JSON round trip, surface merge; Blender tests for the MIX demo and Surface mode (5.2.3 and 5.3.0). Colour-mixing renders in docs/renders.
- Limits: an absorbed particle keeps its original fluid id (cohesion and per-fluid parameters stay with it); viscosity and heat conduction are not composition-aware; surface mode is ragged for blobs under about 4 spacings, so the lamp wax stays spheres.

## 0.3.2
- Fill: analytic level-set fill (stratified by density, exact level) plus a 10-step settle is the default; Pour fill keeps the full dynamic fill; Quick fill 3 steps. Faster than the 0.3.1 dynamic fill (see docs/TECHNICAL.md).
- Wax blobs: cohesive immiscible phases couple their own particles' velocities, lava lamp wax starts as three blobs inside the cone, cold wax denser / warm wax lighter than the carrier; tests for connectivity, interface mixing, rise and sink, two blobs.
- Lamp render: faint blue liquid volume, glossy blobs, lights hidden from the camera and the glass reflections.
- Combustion fill test for cone and sphere tanks (the combustion world already filled the real cavity).

## 0.3.1
- Fluid mode: Fill / Settle phase. Container fluids are seeded from the cavity's real shape through the collision-body projection (lowest point first along gravity), poured and settled dynamically under the Environment gravity (or Quick fill); the settled state is the start frame and heat starts at "Start heat at frame". Capacity check with overflow report (open vessel spills, sealed vessel reports over-pressure), layer heights and hydrostatic pressure report.
- Confinement safety: escaped particles are projected back inside and counted; tests for cone, sphere, tilted cylinder, vase-shaped cavity.
- Lava lamp demo: carrier and three waxes fill the cone and settle into layers; clearer glass; renders re-done. Particle scale now applies to the rendered radius.
- Combustion mode keeps its own fill path (documented in docs/TECHNICAL.md).

## 0.3.0
- Fluid mode (panel switch Combustion | Fluid): volumetric, particle-based (Lagrangian) fluid dynamics for general fluids. FLIP/PIC liquids with variable-density projection and Boussinesq convection, Archimedes gas parcels, heat sources and conduction, LOTBCBP containers and colliders (moving walls drive the fluid), Environment-driven gravity, barometric pressure, ambient density, wind and gusts, ground friction.
- Assign Selected as Fluid, fluid presets (water, oils, lava-lamp wax, air, helium, CO2, honey), Lava Lamp / Free Gas Plume / Elevated Gas Tower demos, Geometry Nodes groups (per fluid, mesh per particle).
- New tests (NumPy solver, headless Blender on 5.2.3 and 5.3.0) and renders in docs/renders.

## 0.2.0
- Renamed to Tessera Projection (method: LOTBCBP, Level Of Tessellation Boolean Collision Body Projection).
- Projection Remesh: quad remesh driven by the mesh's own projection (crease and Arc Edge density, symmetry plane detected at any position and rotation, UV/attribute transfer, normals tool, resolution multiplier 0.01 to 12, large-mesh fast path with progress and cancel).
- Boolean projection: Fluid Cavity union and joint cuts.
- Environment card: Earth, Mars, Venus, Titan, Jupiter and editable gas mixes.
- Systems: multi-system detection, twin-engine crossfeed demo, interactive-physics context scan.
- Tested on Blender 5.2.3 LTS and 5.3.0.
